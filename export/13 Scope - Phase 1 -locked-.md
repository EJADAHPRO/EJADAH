# Ejadah app — Phase 1 scope (locked 28 Jul 2026)

Supersedes §17 of the build prompt. Build everything IN, nothing OUT.

## Navigation (revised — exams cut frees a tab)
Home · Learn · Career · People · Profile

## IN
**Learning** — recorded courses (catalogue, detail, player, progress) · handouts · flashcards (spaced repetition) · course quizzes
**Career** — postgraduate database (199 real records) · programme detail · recognition flags (EG/AE/SA/QA) · comparison up to 3 · shortlist · 23 country guides · roadmap generator · what-if scenarios
**Marketplaces** — tutoring · mentoring · consulting · tutor onboarding (6 steps) · tutor earnings (70/30 itemised) · instructor dashboard · my bookings
**Profile** — NFC card · NFC editing · public profile · certificates · certificate verification
**Platform** — notifications · membership & pricing · checkout/billing (web only, app redirects) · admin panel (web only)

## OUT of Phase 1
Course AI assistant · Live Academy · live classroom · clinical demos · dental library · research hub ·
ALL licensing exams (hub, question bank, mock exams, free tests, analytics, exam dashboard) ·
accreditations · private/group training · achievements · dental AI assistant · community · global search

## Standing decisions
- Roadmap generator lives in the Career tab, with a CTA card on Home.
- Programme validation deferred: recognition flags render "Not verified" until sourced. Not a blocker for Phase 1 build.
- 150 of 199 deadlines are expired — the database hides expired by default.
- Numerals: Western (0–9) in both languages.
- Arabic heading auto-reduce: 12% above 28 characters.
- Payments never in-app: bottom sheet redirects to ejadah.com checkout.
