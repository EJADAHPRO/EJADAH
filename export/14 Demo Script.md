# Ejadah App — Demo Script (10–12 min)
Open `Ejadah App - Home & Career.dc.html`. Left rail = who's using it + language + screen state. Phone is fully tappable.

## 0. Opening (30s)
"One app, six tabs, fully bilingual — flip عربي and back: direction, fonts, numerals and even the gradient angle mirror instantly." (Tap عربي, then EN.)

## 1. Student (2 min)
Pick **Dental student**. Home CTA reads "Struggling with a subject? A TA, weekly" → tap **Find a tutor**.
- Show filters: All Subjects/Pre-Clinical/Clinical/Exam Prep with live counts, Year 1–5 row, ⚡Available-now + staff-only toggles, search "anatomy".
- Open **Dr. Yasmine Nour** → three areas: About (what you get, quals, policies) · **Packages** (select Monthly — "4 sessions — one per week for a month", CTA under it updates) · Reviews.
- Tap Book → walk the 8 steps fast (Subject → Duration price updates → Date/time — try Continue without picking to show validation toast → Goal → Files warning → Format → Review → Payment) → "Session booked" with reference.
- My bookings: balance card "3 of 4 sessions left" → **Book from your balance** → payment shows "From balance — EGP 0 due".

## 2. Fresh graduate (2 min)
Persona **Fresh graduate** → Home CTA "Talk to someone who made the move" → Mentoring.
- Stats strip 80+/2,400+/4.8★/90%. Area chips (International Career…). Open **Dr. Youssef Ramadan**: 58 mentees · 94% goal rate · Programs tab → 3 Months MOST POPULAR EGP 5,500 (EGP 458/session) → Reviews (142) with outcome-labelled reviewers ("Now working in UK").

## 3. GP dentist (1.5 min)
Persona **GP dentist** → "Complex case? Get a written specialist opinion" → Consulting.
- 48h turnaround stat, category chips, deliverables on every card. Open **Dr. Mona Khalil** → Session Types (Career Strategy 550 · CV Review 400 · Mock Interview 500 · Checklist 280) → guarantee line "Written deliverable included…".

## 4. Specialist (1.5 min)
Persona **Specialist** → "Your next qualification, honestly priced" → Postgrad.
- 199 real programmes, USD prices, Open/Closing counts, expired hidden honestly. Open the **King's featured card** → the nine-section deep profile (flip to عربي here — fully translated). Back → save 2 programmes → Compare.

## 5. Career roadmap (2 min)
Career tab → Start the roadmap → answer the 5 steps (pick "No money to spend" to show honesty) → **Build my route**: generating lines → result: best route + fit %, two alternatives, 5 costed stages, WATCH OUT, START THIS MONTH → tap a What-if ("I have more money") — the whole route recalculates. (Offline it falls back to a worked example with a quiet note — never an error.)

## 6. Supply side (1.5 min)
Left rail **Tutor · supply side** → lands on **Booking requests**: accept one (toast), edit availability, save.
- Earnings: EGP 8,400 balance, weekly chart, per-session 70/30 itemised.
- Connect hub → Teach on Ejadah → the 8-step application (jump via rail: Pricing shows live 70/30; Verification shows Syndicate card + agreement) → Submit → "Application submitted!" timeline.

## 7. Close (30s)
Profile → **Certificates**: Ejadah-verified vs self-reported, Syndicate ✓ / request ADA · QCHP → public verify page. "Everything you saw is bilingual, personalized by persona, and remembers what you did — flashcards due dropped because we reviewed them earlier."
