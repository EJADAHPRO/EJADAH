PHASE 4 DELIVERABLE · 30 JULY 2026

Revenue map — every trigger point on the journey

Nine trigger moments, each naming what the user just did, what to offer, and why that instant rather than any other. Nothing here is a new product — every offer is something Ejadah already sells.

Why five stages and not Phase 2's seven: this map covers only the stages where money changes hands. Phase 2's first touch, signup and referral are deliberately absent — no offer should fire before value has been delivered, and the referral ask is a favour, not a sale. Renewal is added here because the January decision has no equivalent on the Phase 2 axis.

THE RULE THAT KEEPS THIS FROM FEELING GREEDY
Gate output and scale, never access to truth. Fees, deadlines, recognition status, exam requirements and cancellation terms stay free forever — that is the trust moat and the reason anyone believes you over a Facebook group. Sell the work done on top of it: the teaching, the person, the document, the reminder at scale.

IMPACT HPOLICY, NOT CODE
NOT A TRIGGER
Cold launch · app open · empty state
No offer fires here, deliberately. An ask before any value has been delivered is the fastest way to be uninstalled — and it teaches users to dismiss every later prompt.
RAIL 1 · COURSES — DIGITAL
In-app purchase · 15–30% to the store
Apple §3.1.1 forbids an external checkout link for digital content, so this rail costs you the platform fee and there is no legal way around it. It is also the only rail that scales without anyone's time — which is why trigger 3 matters more than its impact rating suggests.
RAIL 2 · SESSIONS — HUMAN SERVICE
ejadah.com checkout · 30% margin
Real-world services between people, so external payment is permitted and you keep the full 70/30 split. Highest revenue per transaction, but bounded by how many hours your tutors have.
RAIL 3 · CARD — PHYSICAL GOODS
ejadah.com checkout · EGP 299
Shipped goods, so store rules don't apply. Small ticket, but every card sold is a walking billboard that carries your branding into a clinic — it earns twice.
FIVE THINGS I WOULD NOT MONETISE, THOUGH YOU COULD
Deadline alerts
The single feature that keeps the app installed between actions. Charging for it would trade retention for pennies.
Recognition & fees data
This is the trust moat. Paywalling verified facts makes you the thing you are replacing.
The first roadmap
It is the activation event. Anything charged before it kills the funnel at its narrowest point.
Certificate verification
A third party checking a credential is free marketing arriving at your door. Never charge the visitor.
The public profile
Your only organic growth loop. Every gate on it is a gate on your own distribution.
