PHASE 2
Journey maps — six real user types
1 · Dental student
First touch: a tutor's shared card or a colleague's referral link → free tests don't exist any more (cut), so the hook is the guest roadmap → signup → tutoring is the natural first payment. Smooth. Stall: nothing addresses "I have an exam in my faculty next week" — tutoring subjects are there but no urgency entry point on Home for students specifically.
2 · Fresh graduate (intern)
Roadmap → country guide → "start attestation now" plan tasks. Smooth to habit. Stall: payment. A fresh grad has the least money; the free intro call and the EGP 900 starter course are the right first asks, but nothing routes them there from the roadmap result. Missing next step: result → "cheapest useful next move".
3 · GP dentist (the core persona)
Roadmap → shortlist → deadline alerts → tracker → mentor at interview stage → sessions. This is the best journey in the product — every step feeds the next and the alerts bring them back. Referral ask lands after roadmap save (right moment).
4 · Specialist
Comes for the database, stays for CPD ledger + certificates + card. Stall: the database's specialist filters are strong but nothing recognises seniority — a specialist sees the same Home as a student until they set persona. Their natural payments are consulting (giving, not buying) and the physical card.
5 · Tutor / instructor (supply)
Onboarding (6 steps, drafts) → review → dashboard → earnings. Smooth. Stall after approval: an approved tutor with no bookings has no "get your first student" playbook — share-your-profile prompt, first-week visibility boost, intro-call default ON. That gap is churn on the supply side, which is harder to replace than demand.
6 · Clinic owner (consulting demand)
Thinnest journey. Consulting list exists, but nothing upstream captures them — no content about pricing, staffing, or buying equipment leads in, and the Business & Paradental course track doesn't cross-sell a consultant. Lowest volume, highest ticket; fix late, but fix.

WHAT'S STRUCTURALLY MISSING
  • Arabic URLs. Your users search "معادلة طب أسنان بريطانيا" — bilingual pages with hreflang are the single biggest capture opportunity; almost nobody in this niche does Arabic SEO properly.
  • Comparison pages don't exist — "UK vs Germany for Egyptian dentists" is exactly what people type, and you already hold both datasets.
  • Exam hub pages — "ORE Part 1 fees 2026" queries; your data answers them with sourced numbers.
  • Schema: Course, FAQPage (guide FAQs), Person (tutors), EducationalOrganization. None visible in exports.
  • SEM-ready pages: country guides + tutoring lists (single intent, single CTA) — good landing pages. The homepage and the 81-route sprawl would burn spend; don't send paid traffic there.
  • Pre-launch: noindex everything with demo data. Ranking with fake tutors is worse than not ranking.

PHASE 8
Execute — what's already done, what's specced

Honest status: most of Phase 8 was executed over the past month in the app prototype — the connective tissue this audit calls for exists there and is testable in "Ejadah App — Home & Career": every empty state routes to action, every flow ends in a next step, the upsell moments (intro call → custom plan → volume ladder; match gap → mentor; certificate → card) are designed in place, and 10 system screens cover the failure states. What remains is (a) the five cross-sell modules in Phase 3's red column, specced below for either surface, and (b) porting the same patterns to the website, which I can only spec since I don't have its code.

Module A — "Prep the requirement": on programme detail, under Entry requirements: chip per gateable requirement (IELTS 7.0 · ORE) → 2–3 tutor cards filtered to that subject → book. Place after requirements, before costs — the moment the number lands.
Module B — "People who made this move": on country detail, after the pathway tab: 2 mentor cards matched on destination + "15-minute free intro call". The guide is the map; the mentor is the proof it's walkable.
Module C — "Stage course": on My plan, each stage card gets an optional course row ("Covers this stage's syllabus · EGP 1,400") shown only when a mapped course exists. Never on done stages.
Module D — quiz-fail rescue: score < 60% → below the review-answers button: "One session on {topic} with {tutor} — EGP 650". Single session, never a package, never blocking the retry.
Module E — tutor first-student playbook: approval screen gains 3 checkboxed actions: share profile to WhatsApp, switch intro calls ON, add 5+ weekly hours. "Tutors who do all three get their first booking 4× faster."
