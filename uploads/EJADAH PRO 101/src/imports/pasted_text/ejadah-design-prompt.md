# EJADAH INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY

## Complete Figma Product Design and Brand System Prompt

Design a complete, modern, premium, minimal, scalable digital platform for **Ejadah International Academy**, a Cairo-based international dental education and professional-development company.

Use the supplied **Ejadah International Academy logo** as the exact visual source of truth.

Do not redesign, distort, rotate, recolor, simplify, or alter the proportions of the original logo. Build the entire brand language, interface system, typography, gradients, illustrations, icons, cards, motion, and visual hierarchy around the logo’s distinctive warm colors and curved geometry.

Brand slogan:

**“Bridging the Gap Beyond Borders.”**

Supporting brand idea:

**International dental education, career development, professional connection, and clinical knowledge in one intelligent ecosystem.**

The platform should feel like a unique combination of:

* A premium dental streaming platform
* A modern learning management system
* An exam-preparation platform
* A professional dental network
* A tutoring and consultation marketplace
* An international dental career portal
* A research and knowledge platform
* An AI-powered study assistant

The usability may take inspiration from modern streaming, educational, professional-networking, and marketplace platforms, but the visual identity must remain completely original and unmistakably Ejadah.
